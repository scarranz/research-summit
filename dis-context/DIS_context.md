> **Source discipline.** Company-disclosed figures (10-K, 10-Q, earnings releases) are ground truth and are labeled with their fiscal period. Management targets from earnings calls are labeled **[GUIDANCE]**. Where a metric is only available in one document family (e.g., subscriber counts live in the earnings release, not the 10-Q), that is flagged. Quarter-standalone cash-flow figures for 2Q/3Q FY26 are **derived** (YTD minus prior YTD) because the 10-Q reports cumulative cash flows — labeled **[derived]**.

FY2026 is a 53-week year** (the extra "53rd week" falls in Q4 FY26, worth ~+1.5–2% of revenue and ~+4% at the OI level — management guides EPS growth both incl. and excl. it). ## 2. The Investment Narrative (cross-quarter arc)

Five threads define the Disney story across FY25–FY26:

1. **Streaming crossed into structural profitability.** Entertainment SVOD (Disney+, Hulu SVOD) operating income went from a ~$4B loss three years prior to **$1.3B in FY2025**, with SVOD margin climbing **~5% → 10% target → 12–13% delivered** through Q1–Q3 FY26. The strategy hardened from "fold Hulu into Disney+" into "**Disney+ as the digital centerpiece**" of the company — now layered with OpenAI/Sora licensing, a TikTok distribution deal, vertical video ("Verts"), an explored free AVOD tier, and a 2027 games/merchandise ecosystem — all aimed at **churn reduction and lifetime value**.

2. **EPS growth is the anchor and it got sharper.** From "double-digit" FY26 to an explicit **~12% adjusted EPS growth (ex-53rd week) / ~16% (incl.)** — with **double-digit FY2027** reaffirmed at every call — held through a CEO transition and some box-office misses (*Mandalorian & Grogu*, live-action *Moana* softness flagged for Q4 FY26).

3. **ESPN executed a real-time DTC pivot.** DTC app announced (Aug 2025) → NFL deal (NFL Network + RedZone for a **10% ESPN equity stake**) + WWE Premium Live Events → **flagship launch Aug 21, 2025** ($29.99 "Trio" bundle of Disney+/Hulu/ESPN) → ~80% of new ESPN subs took the Trio → NFL Network deal **closed** Q1 FY26 → heading into ESPN's **first Super Bowl** (inventory sold out). Sports subscriber declines improved from −7/−8% to −4%.

4. **Experiences is the profit ballast and re-accelerated.** Record OI essentially every quarter; domestic per-caps +8% (Q3'25), attendance returning to **+3–4% growth** by Q3'26 as the Universal *Epic Universe* headwind lapped. Cruise fleet expanding **8 → 13 ships by 2031**; a multi-year (~$60B-framed) parks capex cycle is underway (Abu Dhabi, Villains Land, Avengers Campus, Paris' World of Frozen). Persistent soft spot: the **Asia consumer (Shanghai / Hong Kong)**.

5. **Capital return accelerated; M&A stance flipped from "buy nothing" to "prune."** Buyback ramped **$3.5B (FY25) → ≥$9B (FY26)**; dividend +50%. Management repeatedly declined to chase WBD/media M&A ("we don't need to do anything"), instead **monetizing non-core assets (the A+E stake sale)** to fund buybacks.

## 3. Segment Deep Dives

### 3.1 Entertainment
Non-sports film & episodic content, streaming, and linear networks.

**What's inside:**
- **Linear Networks** — ABC + 8 owned stations (~20% US household reach); Disney, Freeform, FX, National Geographic (73%-owned); ~180 international channels. Includes the (now being sold) A+E stake.
- **Direct-to-Consumer (DTC/SVOD)** — **Disney+** (global) + **Hulu** (US, now 100%-owned). The international "Star" tile was rebranded **Hulu** in Oct 2025.
- **Content Sales/Licensing & Other** — theatrical, TV/VOD, home entertainment, Disney Theatrical (Broadway), Disney Music, ILM/Skywalker Sound, 30% Tata Play stake.

**FY2025 (10-K):** Revenue $42,466M (+3%); segment OI **$4,674M (+19%)**. Legacy sub-lines (retired for reporting after FY25):
| | Rev FY25 | YoY | OI FY25 | OI FY24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Linear Networks | $9,364M | (12%) | $2,955M | $3,452M |
| Direct-to-Consumer | $24,614M | +8% | **$1,327M** | **$143M** |
| Content Sales/Licensing & Other | $8,488M | +10% | $392M | $328M |

**Reporting change:** From FY2026 Disney reports Entertainment by **revenue type** (Subscription & affiliate fees / Advertising / Content sales / Other), not the old Linear/DTC/Content split. **Consumer Products moves from Experiences into Entertainment starting Q1 FY27.**

**FY26 quarterly OI:** 1Q26 $1,100M (−35%, weak theatrical comp) → 2Q26 $1,336M (+6%) → **3Q26 $1,680M (+64%)**, driven by subscription & affiliate fee growth and streaming margin.

### 3.2 Sports (ESPN)
ESPN (generally 80%-owned by Disney, moving toward ~72% after the NFL deal — Hearst 18%, NFL 10%).

- **DTC (Aug 2025 launch):** **ESPN Unlimited** (full flagship — all linear channels + DTC) and **ESPN Select** (formerly ESPN+).
- **Rights:** NFL, NBA (ramping — a major FY26 cost/step-up driver), MLB, NHL, college football/CFP, **WWE Premium Live Events (exclusive from Sept 2025)**, soccer, tennis, golf. UFC rights **expired Dec 2025** (a Sports "other revenue" headwind in FY26). Sportsbook partner switched from **ESPN BET (PENN) → DraftKings** effective Dec 1, 2025.

**FY2025 (10-K):** Revenue $17,672M (flat); segment OI **$2,882M (+20%)** — but the YoY jump is largely the **Star India deconsolidation** (into the Reliance JV); ESPN domestic OI was actually **−8%** on higher rights costs.

**FY26 quarterly OI:** 1Q26 $191M (−23%) → 2Q26 $652M (−5%) → 3Q26 $858M (−17%). OI pressured all year by rising programming/rights costs (NBA ramp, NBA playoff sweeps in Q3), partly offset by the NFL Network contribution.
[GUIDANCE] FY26 Sports OI raised from low- to **mid-single-digit growth** (ex-53rd week) after folding in the NFL Network transaction.

### 3.3 Experiences
Domestic & international parks, Disney Cruise Line, Disney Vacation Club, and Consumer Products.

**FY2025 (10-K):** Revenue $36,156M (+6%); segment OI **$9,995M (+8%)**.
| | Rev FY25 | OI FY25 | OI YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parks & Experiences – Domestic | $25,191M | $6,375M | +8% |
| Parks & Experiences – International | $6,520M | $1,442M | +6% |
| Consumer Products | $4,445M | $2,178M | +7% |

Domestic per-capita spend +5%; domestic attendance −1% (FY25).

**Cruise expansion:** 6 ships in FY25 (Magic, Wonder, Dream, Fantasy, Wish, Treasure) → adding **Disney Destiny** (first sail Nov 20, 2025) and **Disney Adventure** (~200k tons, first sail Mar 2026, homeported in Singapore — largest ship ever) → **fleet to 13 ships by 2031** (a ~40% capacity increase already in progress; OLC/Japan ship ~2029 on a royalty model).

**Capital plan:** FY26 capex **~$9B** (10-K quantifies only near-term capex; the widely-cited **~$60B / 10-year** Experiences framing is used by management on calls but is *not restated as a hard dollar commitment in the 10-K*). Pipeline: Abu Dhabi park with Miral (capital-light, licensing/royalty), Villains Land (Orlando), Avengers Campus expansion (Anaheim), World of Frozen / Disney Adventure World (Paris, 2026), DinoLand → "Tropical Americas" (2027).

**FY26 quarterly OI:** 1Q26 **$3,309M** (+6%) → 2Q26 $2,615M (+5%) → 3Q26 $3,017M (+20%). Q3 helped by ~$100M of tariff refunds (immaterial for full year). Watch item: **weaker Asia consumer (Shanghai & Hong Kong)** in Q3, continuing into Q4.
[GUIDANCE] FY26 Experiences OI at the **high end of high-single-digit growth** (ex-53rd week).

## 5. Streaming / DTC — The Profitability Inflection

### 5.1 Entertainment SVOD Operating-Income Ramp
Disney now reports streaming economics via the **Entertainment SVOD operating income / margin** framework (Disney+, Hulu SVOD; ex-Hulu Live TV/Fubo). This is the single most important trend line in the story.

| ($M) | Q1 FY25 | Q2 FY25 | Q3 FY25 | Q4 FY25 | **FY25** | Q1 FY26 | Q2 FY26 | Q3 FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total SVOD revenue | 4,816 | 4,862 | 4,972 | 5,081 | **19,731** | 5,346 | 5,486 | 5,532 |
| **SVOD operating income** | 261 | 310 | 329 | 402 | **1,302** | 450 | **582** | **712** |
| **SVOD operating margin** | 5.4% | 6.4% | 6.6% | 7.9% | **6.6%** | 8.4% | **10.6%** | **12.9%** |

**Takeaway:** SVOD margin roughly doubled from ~5–8% across FY25 to ~11–13% by mid-FY26 — driven by rate increases, subscriber/volume growth (incl. international wholesale), churn reduction from the integrated Disney+/Hulu app, and cost discipline. 2Q26 was the "first double-digit Entertainment SVOD operating margin." Management benchmark (D'Amaro): *"compared to where Netflix was at a similar revenue scale, we look quite similar in terms of margins."*
[GUIDANCE] FY26: ≥10% SVOD margin (delivered ahead of plan); margin gains to come "in chunks, not basis points" beyond FY26.

### 5.3 Streaming strategy levers
- **App unification:** Hulu fully integrated into Disney+ as one app; full unification (incl. live TV) targeted for **~end of calendar 2026**. Bundled subs churn materially less.
- **Bundles:** "Trio" = Disney+/Hulu/ESPN Unlimited at $29.99 (lowest churn, highest LTV); also a Disney+/Hulu/HBO Max bundle (very sticky).
- **New surfaces:** vertical video ("Verts"), Creators Collection short-form, OpenAI/Sora character licensing (3-yr paid deal, ~250 characters), a **TikTok distribution deal** (announced Aug 2026), and an **explored free AVOD tier** as top-of-funnel (nothing announced). Games/merchandise ecosystem rollout targeted **spring 2027**.

## 7. Strategic & Corporate Actions

- **Hulu — now 100% Disney-owned.** Disney paid NBCU $8.6B (Dec 2023) at the guaranteed floor + ~$0.4B incremental (FY25) after appraisal. The close drove the **~$3.3B non-cash tax benefit** in FY25; Disney pays NBCU 50% of future amortization tax benefits over ~15 years starting FY26.
- **ESPN / NFL Transaction** (announced Oct 2025, **closed Q1 FY26**): ESPN acquired NFL Network, RedZone, and NFL Fantasy in exchange for a **10% NFL equity stake in ESPN** (Disney effective ownership ~72%). NFL game windows expand 22 → 28. Estimated ~$0.03 dilutive to FY26 adjusted EPS; accretive thereafter.
- **Fubo / Live-TV JV** (Oct 29, 2025): Disney combined Hulu Live TV with FuboTV; Disney holds **70%**. Drove a non-cash $307M tax charge in 1Q26.
- **India JV** (Nov 14, 2024): Star India + Disney+ Hotstar merged with Reliance media; **Disney owns 37%**, now equity-method — the deconsolidation is the main driver of FY25's optically strong Sports OI and drove a −$202M equity loss.
- **A+E stake sale** (announced Aug 2026): monetizing the ~50% A+E Global Media investment; proceeds (~$1.2B) directed to buyback. (Related impairments hit restructuring: $147M in 2Q26, $812M in 3Q26.)
- **ESPN full DTC launch** (Aug 21, 2025): ESPN Unlimited + ESPN Select; ~80% of new ESPN subs took the Trio bundle.
- **OpenAI/Sora** (3-yr paid license) and **TikTok** (Aug 2026) distribution deals; Epic Games partnership.
- **Leadership:** Bob Iger → **Josh D'Amaro** as CEO (~May 2026); Hugh Johnston stays CFO. Framing shifted to a "One Disney" operating model with strategy continuity.

## 8. Key Risk Factors (from 10-K, ranked by materiality)

1. **Cord-cutting / linear decline** — Linear Networks revenue −12% YoY; goodwill/intangible impairment risk; DTC accelerates linear erosion.
2. **DTC competition & profitability durability** — 10-K: "no assurance the DTC model will be or remain profitable."
3. **Carriage renewals / blackouts** — **Disney channels pulled from YouTube TV Oct 30, 2025** (blackout, impact not estimable); multiple MVPD renewals in FY26.
4. **Sports-rights cost inflation** — NFL/NBA/college rights driving ESPN domestic OI down despite revenue growth.
5. **Parks/Experiences cyclicality** — recession/discretionary-spend sensitivity, FX, weather, plus large fixed capex commitments (cruise ships, parks).
6. **Macro & inflation** — labor cost inflation (parks CBAs), rates, softening consumer (notably Asia).
7. **FX translation & inbound tourism.**
8. **Content taste risk** — large upfront bets before knowing acceptance; theatrical below pre-COVID.
9. **Strategic-change execution** — Fubo, ESPN/NFL, India JV write-down risk.
10. **Labor disputes** — several guild CBAs expire FY26.
11. **Cybersecurity & data.**
12. **Regulatory/legal/IP** — FCC, privacy, tariffs, AI legal uncertainty, pending securities class action, copyright expirations (Steamboat Willie).

## 10. Earnings-Call Commentary — Highlights & Quotes

**Q3 FY25 (Aug 6, 2025 · Iger/Johnston):** NFL deal + WWE PLEs announced; ESPN DTC launch set for Aug 21 at $29.99 Trio; WDW record Q3 revenue, domestic per-caps +8%. *Iger: "We don't really look at being in the linear business and the streaming business. We're in the television business."* *Johnston: "We certainly don't intend to stop at 10% margin... through a growth-oriented strategy, not a cost-oriented strategy."*

**Q4/FY25 (Nov 13, 2025 · Iger/Johnston):** FY25 adjusted EPS +19%; DTC OI $1.3B ($300M ahead of guide); FY26 buyback $7B (2×), dividend +50% to $1.50; ESPN DTC ~80% Trio attach. *Iger: "Just three years ago, our DTC business was running a $4 billion operating loss."* On M&A: *"We don't need to do anything."*

**Q1 FY26 (Feb 2, 2026 · Iger/Johnston):** Streaming 12% revenue growth, >50% earnings growth; Experiences quarterly revenue >$10B first time; NFL deal closed. *Iger on WBD: "We don't really feel that we have a need to buy more IP."*

**Q2 FY26 (May 6, 2026 · D'Amaro's first call/Johnston):** Explicit **12% FY26 adjusted EPS** guide; SVOD hit 12% margin; cruise plan 8→13 ships by 2031; domestic attendance −1% (international-visitation headwind). *D'Amaro: "We're building Disney+ to serve as the immersive interactive digital centerpiece of the company."* *Johnston: "We're generating more revenue at Disney Entertainment in streaming than in linear, more than double."*

**Q3 FY26 (Aug 5, 2026 · D'Amaro/Johnston):** Total segment OI +21%; Experiences record ($10B rev, +10%; attendance +3%, per-cap +4%); SVOD margin 13%; buyback raised to ≥$9B; A+E sale + TikTok deal announced; Asia consumer soft. *D'Amaro: "With 4% per cap growth, we're certainly not discounting our way to volume growth."* *D'Amaro: "compared to where Netflix was at a similar revenue scale, we look quite similar in terms of margins."*
