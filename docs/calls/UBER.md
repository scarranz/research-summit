## Q4 2025 — February 4, 2026

## Analysis (per the EARNINGS_CONVENTIONS Part I rules — fact → why → so-what)

**The take:** the print is clean — trips at a 15B annual run rate, 200M+ MAUs, GB +22%, $9.8B FCF
(+42%) — but the whole call is a pre-emptive defense of the AV thesis. Management spent its capital
answering the winner-take-most bear case, and the most load-bearing new disclosure isn't a growth
rate, it's a geography fact: ~75% of US profits sit OUTSIDE the top-20 cities AVs will address for
years. Meanwhile the CFO seat changes hands mid-quarter.

1. **Trips accelerated to a 15B annual run rate; MAPC growth stepped from 14% → 18% over 2025** —
   why: Balaji attributes it to audience (not price), with new rider/eater cohorts retaining
   *better* than prior US cohorts on deliberate early-lifecycle investment; frequency growing
   slower purely as cohorts mature → so-what: the growth is trips-led and structural, which is the
   healthiest input mix — the durability question shifts from "is price tapped out?" to "how deep
   into the 450M annual-active base can penetration go?" `thesis`
2. **~75% of US profits come from markets OUTSIDE the top-20 cities, and those are growing faster
   than the top 20** — why: Balaji volunteered this to refute the "profit pools concentrated in
   AV-target cities" bear case; 70% of the US and 60% of mobility GB (international) sit beyond
   AV's near-term reach → so-what: this reframes AV risk — even a fast robotaxi ramp in dense cores
   touches a minority of Uber's profit. Candor-against-the-narrative; weight it. `thesis`
3. **AVs on Uber run ~30% higher trips/vehicle/day than 1P standalone, and SF/Austin/Atlanta GB
   *accelerated*** — why: Uber added 50× the AV industry's trip volume last year and its demand
   density smooths the Sat→Mon ~45% trough that idle robotaxis can't fill → so-what: management is
   converting the existential AV question into a utilization argument where scale is the moat —
   but it's still an assertion pending third-party-verifiable economics. `watch`
4. **Insurance is turning from "deleveraging to leverage" for the first time since COVID** — why:
   reform wins (California UM/UIM cut from $1M to $60K/$300K) plus safety tech drove hundreds of
   millions in savings, letting Uber hold US prices flat → so-what: price consistency compounds
   long-term elasticity — the mechanism behind the guided US acceleration into 2026, and a genuine
   tailwind reversal, not a one-off. `thesis`
5. **Capital-return philosophy reaffirmed as "aggressive buyers," share count down "a healthy
   amount," but the explicit 50%-of-FCF line was softened** — why: Balaji framed reinvestment +
   AV + buyback as "not a trade-off" on ~$10B FCF; pressed by Nowak on the 50% commitment, he
   pivoted to "continue at a steady cadence" → so-what: a subtle loosening of a prior hard
   commitment as AV capital calls loom — track whether buyback pace actually holds. `tone`
6. **Delivery advertising penetration has passed the old 2% ceiling, with enterprise ad growth now
   outpacing SMB** — why: SMB penetration already well above 2% and enterprise "playing catch-up,"
   plus nascent grocery/retail and mobility ad surfaces → so-what: the 2% target that framed the ad
   TAM is now a floor, not a ceiling — a high-margin lever with fresh runway. `curious`
7. **Delivery growth hit multi-year highs on five named drivers (selection, less-dense, grocery/
   retail, membership, new international)** — why: selection still only 30-40% of addressable in
   many markets; UK #1 organic, Germany neck-and-neck, Japan #1 → so-what: the "delivery is mature"
   bear case keeps losing — organic category gains without buying share is the tell that the
   platform flywheel is real. `thesis`
8. **CFO transition: Prashanth Mahendra-Rajah steps down Feb 16 "to serve America," Balaji
   Krishnamurthy elevated** — why: framed as an exciting personal opportunity, seamless handoff →
   so-what: continuity risk is low (Balaji ran IR/strategic finance), but a CFO change at the exact
   moment AV capital-allocation discipline matters most is worth watching. `watch`

**Seeds → Q1 2026 Watch List:** the 15-cities-by-YE-2026 AV rollout pace and any Waymo/Tesla
share impact · whether the 50%-of-FCF buyback cadence actually holds under AV capital calls ·
insurance "leverage" flowing to US acceleration · delivery ad penetration past 2% · Balaji's first
solo quarter as CFO.
