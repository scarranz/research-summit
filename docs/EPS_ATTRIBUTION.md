# EPS Attribution (Market Analysis, third tab)

What drove the index this year: aggregate EPS revisions vs the multiple, by security / industry group / sector,
for the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq-100, with any group removable (ex-semiconductors, ex-IT, ex-GOOGL/AMZN, any ticker).

## 1. Method

`q = weight / price` (implied index shares; the index-size constant cancels in every ratio).
Index price `I = Σ q·P`, index earnings `E = Σ q·EPS`, multiple `PE = I / E`.
Between two snapshots, with `q` fixed at the start: `1 + R = (1 + g)(1 + m)` — `g = E₁/E₀ − 1` aggregate EPS revision,
`m = PE₁/PE₀ − 1`. A security contributes `q·ΔEPS / E₀` to `g` and `q·ΔP / I₀` to `R`; multiple = price − EPS.
All of it adds exactly over any group. Forward growth (CY2026 → CY2027) is the same `E` at one snapshot.

**Aggregate vs weighted average.** The aggregate (earnings-share weighted = `weight / PE`) is what reconciles with the
index P/E. The cap-weighted average of stock growth weights by market cap, so high-multiple names count more than the
earnings they bring (the gap is a mix effect). Both and the median are shown side by side in the page.

## 2. Corrections to `Index_Concentration_EPS_Analysis_Spec.md`

1. **Spec §5.2 aggregates `Σ (weight/price) × total net income`.** Dimensionally wrong: `weight/price` is index shares,
   total net income is a whole company, so it mixes company scales (and counts GOOG + GOOGL's identical net income twice).
   The right pair is `weight/price × EPS` (or `weight/market cap × net income`). Same snapshot, S&P forward growth CY26→27:
   $-earnings method 31.2% vs EPS method 22.0% (Oct 16); the spec's own table (26.4%) is a third number. The page uses EPS.
2. **Rows that silently fell out:** GOOG (no per-share EPS; now takes GOOGL's), BRK/B, ASML, ERIE, FDX (early snapshots).
   Futures `NQM6/NQU6/NQZ6 Index` sit in the QQQ file and are now dropped like `USD Curncy`.
3. **Non-USD reporters** (PDD CNY; CCEP, FER, ASML EUR) have EPS in local currency against a USD price — nulled until an FX
   column exists (follow-up: one BDP formula for `FX_RATE`, not a new time series).
4. **GAAP basis.** `IS_COMP_EPS_GAAP` carries non-operating items: GOOGL CY26 EPS 14.35 → 20.62 in one quarter and above
   its CY27 (15.21); AMZN 8.78 → 12.81 vs CY27 10.78. This inflates the CY2026 revision (≈ +6 pp of the S&P's +17.4% YTD
   comes from GOOGL, GOOG, AMZN). The page warns and ships a one-click "remove GOOGL · GOOG · AMZN". Whether to move to
   BEST_EPS (adjusted) is a data decision for the analyst.
5. Per-stock growth inside the cap-weighted average is capped at ±100% (EPS from ~0 breaks any average).

## 3. Data cleaning (extract_eps.py)

Dropped: `USD Curncy`, `* Index` futures, `MMED US Equity` (all snapshots, agreed), names with no price (`2602335D`).
Kept in weights/concentration but out of the EPS maths: no EPS or non-USD EPS. *Coverage* shows the weight that remains
(S&P ≈ 98%, Nasdaq-100 ≈ 95–97%). A name entering or leaving between two snapshots is outside that period.
