# Private / unreported grocers: caveats and reliability (as of 2026-11-23)

Companion to `private.json`. Reliability grades: A = company or filing; B = trade press reporting company data; C = third-party estimate; D = weak or single local source.

## Cross-cutting
- **Revenue estimates:** the only consistent third-party source is the NRF / Kantar 2026 Top 100 (2025 US retail sales, published Jul 1 2026). NRF says these are estimates and may not match filings. Its **Aldi figure ($58.36B) includes Trader Joe's** (different Aldi family branch) and the acquired Winn-Dixie/Harveys stores. Lidl, Whole Foods and Erewhon are not listed separately.
- **Private-label share of sales:** Numerator panel data via Talk Business (Jan 7 2025): Aldi 80%, Trader Joe's 70%, H-E-B 34%. These are estimates from panel data covering the 12 months to ~Dec 2024, not company figures. Chain Store Age reported H-E-B at 32% of *volume* from the same report.
- **SKU counts** are the weakest field. Only Lidl has an executive-sourced number (3,300 core items). Treat the commonly quoted Aldi (~1,400-1,800) and Trader Joe's (~4,000) counts as folklore: the sources conflict and none comes from the company.
- **Sales per sq ft:** not credibly reported for anyone. Erewhon's $1,800-$2,500 is a repeated, poorly sourced claim (2019 Forbes / LABJ).
- **Margins and comps:** none disclosed, **except The Fresh Market**. Its parent, Cencosud (listed in Chile), reports a USA segment with same-store sales, ticket data, adjusted EBITDA margin and online penetration. This is the most useful hard data in the set.

## Per company
- **Trader Joe's.** Store openings (34 in 2024, 43 in 2025, >20 planned for 2026) come from Grocery Dive citing the company (B). The ~660 store count (Oct 2026) and 631 (Jan 2026) are press or locator-scrape figures (C); the company publishes no total. No credible revenue figure.
- **Aldi US.** Growth plan figures are from Aldi's own Jan 12 2026 release (A): >180 openings in 2026, ~2,800 stores by end-2026, 3,200 by 2028, $9B investment. The ">2,400 at end-2024" figure is press reporting of Aldi's Feb 2025 release (B). "2,614 at Dec 31 2025" is one secondary report (C). Store size: no reliable fleet average (one local report says ~22,000 sq ft; not used).
- **Whole Foods.** US store count of 544 is from the WFM Fast Facts page (undated, 2026). **The Amazon 'Physical stores' line is a proxy, not Whole Foods**: it includes Amazon Fresh and Go (and other Amazon stores) and excludes WFM online orders. Annual 2023/2024/2025 values ($20.0B / $21.2B / $22.6B) are **sums of the quarterly figures in Amazon's earnings releases**. I could not read the 10-K PDF here, so tie them to the 10-K product-group table before publishing. Growth ex-FX ran 4-8% per quarter in 2023-25. Fresh and Go stores are closing from 2026 (some converted to WFM), so later years are not like-for-like. No standalone revenue, margin, private-label share or SKU count.
- **H-E-B.** Store count and sales come from the company's newsroom boilerplate (A) and cover **Texas and Mexico combined**: 460 stores, ">$50B" in 2026 releases (one early-2026 release says 465 / ">$53B", which is inconsistent). The NRF US-only estimate is $44.16B for 2025. Forbes list history: $43.6B (2023), $38.9B (2022). I could not find a 2025 Forbes figure.
- **Erewhon.** 14 locations (Grocery Dive, Sep 2026), up from 10 in Jan 2025. No revenue disclosed. The "40% of revenue from prepared foods + private label" figure is from a 2021 CEO interview. Store sizes are not disclosed.
- **The Fresh Market.** The 3Q25 figures come straight from Cencosud's press release (A): USA SSS -0.2% (3Q24 +0.9%), traffic -2.2%, ticket +2.1%, adj. EBITDA margin 7.5% (vs 7.4%), online 8.5% of sales. The 3Q26 figures come from The Shelby Report's summary of Cencosud's release (B): SSS -0.8%, revenue ~US$500M (+1.4% in USD), margin 7.4% (vs 7.5%), online 9.9%. Store counts conflict between sources (166-172) depending on date and source. The 200-by-2031 target is from a headline only and is unconfirmed. Full-year 2025 USA segment revenue was not extracted, but it is available in Cencosud's 4Q25 materials if needed.
- **Lidl US.** ~3,300 core items and ~80% private label come from CEO Joel Rampoldt's PLMA keynote (Nov 2025) via Discount Retail Consulting (B). About 200 stores as of Nov 2026 (Grocery Dive). No revenue estimate found.

## Gaps (null in the JSON)
SKUs: Trader Joe's, Aldi, Whole Foods, H-E-B, Erewhon, The Fresh Market. Average store size: all except partial or old data (Lidl, Whole Foods). Revenue: Trader Joe's, Whole Foods (standalone), Erewhon, Lidl. Private-label share: Whole Foods (current), Erewhon, The Fresh Market. Sales per sq ft: everyone.
