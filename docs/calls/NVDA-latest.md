Q3 data center revenue increased 21% quarter over quarter to $108 billion, with balanced growth across hyperscale and AI cloud, industrial and enterprise subsegments.

Our hyperscale customers’ cloud revenue growth accelerated for the 6th consecutive quarter. Cloud industry backlog now sits at $2.5 trillion, up from $2 trillion just 3 months ago, underscoring the significant multi-year opportunity ahead of our partners and of NVIDIA.

Proprietary models from OpenAI and Anthropic continue to advance the frontier. With OpenAI launching its latest GPT-6 models and Anthropic releasing its Claude 5.5 models.

As frontier models become more capable, Adoption and revenue are scaling rapidly, with Anthropic’s annualized revenue reaching $81 billion at the end of October. We expect the major frontier labs to contribute approximately 15% of our data center revenue this year.

Open models are accelerating AI adoption, and weekly token volume on OpenRouter has surged 35x year-over-year. 3.5x in just this past 3 months. At NVIDIA, we are committed to fostering a vibrant open model ecosystem so every country, company, and industry can build its own intelligence.

Training partners such as Scale, Mercor, Prime Intellect and Applied Compute are helping enterprises turn open models into their own using their data, tools, and workflows.

NVIDIA compute remains fully utilized across every hyperscaler and neo-cloud we serve. Underscoring the productivity, durability, and fungibility of our infrastructure.

As agents run longer and act with greater autonomy, organizations need stronger controls to ensure safety and security. The NVIDIA Open Agent Safety Platform, which we announced alongside more than 100 partners, enable these controls.

OpenShell contains agents by limiting their access to files and tools, while NVIDIA Sentry running on BlueField-4 monitors the behavior in silicon and stops agents for overstepping limits in milliseconds.

OpenShell is already gaining broad ecosystem support from partners across AI models, enterprise software, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and robotics. Companies working with NVIDIA include Anthropic, AWS, CoreWeave, CrowdStrike, Dell, Google Cloud, HPE, IREN, DeepMind, JPMorgan Chase, Lenovo, Microsoft, Nebius, OpenAI, and SpaceXAI.

To support this massive scale and future growth, Meta extends to expand its deployment of Vera Rubin hardware with the goal of maximizing performance and efficiency for its next generation of AI agents.

Agentic AI is driving extraordinary demand for CPUs, far outstripping supply. NVIDIA Vera CPU meets the growing demand, delivering leading performance at data center processing and scientific simulation workloads.

CoreWeave, Meta, Nscale, and OpenAI have now adopted Vera, joining AWS, OCI, and SpaceXAI, the leading partners we announced last quarter. We continue to see very strong demand for Vera and expect total CPU revenue to more than double in fiscal year ’28.
