## The one-line take

**The print was a clean beat; the call was about the pivots underneath it.** The core thesis got hard confirmation — **NII +23% through a −70bps Fed move**, on balances, not rates — while IBKR quietly reshaped three bets: **prediction markets** (now a multi-venue aggregator, and really a weather/insurance play), **AI** (agentic-trading roadmap, clients already connecting chatbots), and **new markets** (Korea, SpaceX, EU crypto). One yellow flag: **margin loans +67% got only a "we're comfortable" from Peterffy**, and management was candid that the torrid growth isn't getting cheaper to buy.

## Highlights — theme by theme

### 🟢 THESIS CONFIRMED — the rate-cut fear is now empirically dead
- NII rose **+23% YoY to just over $1B** (NIM-table basis $1.1B, +28%) **despite the average US Fed funds rate falling ~70bps (−16%) YoY.** Margin-loan interest +39%, segregated-cash interest +7% — both driven by *balances*, not rates. This is the exact variant view: balance growth out-runs the rate drag.
- **Updated rate sensitivity (the number the Street wanted):** a 25bps Fed move = **±$81M** annual NII (symmetric). ~1/3 of interest-sensitive balances is non-USD; a 25bps non-USD move = **±$38M**. Fully rate-sensitive balances **$28.4B** (vs $22.8B YoY). Starting point June 30, Fed funds effective 3.64%. **Balance growth increases these impacts** — so as balances compound, so does the sensitivity in both directions.
- Subtle treasury shift: portfolio duration stayed **<30 days**, but they **pushed duration out "a bit"** as the US dollar yield curve "finally turned positive in the short-to-medium term." A small change in the rate-positioning playbook worth tracking.

### 🟡 WATCH — margin loans +67% earned only a guarded answer
- Chubak (Wolfe) pressed directly: the margin-balance growth is "extraordinary"; Peterffy has historically disliked fast margin growth — is this healthy/durable or outsized risk-taking?
- **Peterffy: "very cognizant of our client risk on margins… continuously monitoring… we feel comfortable with the current levels."** Brody framed it as a "risk-on environment."
- **Read:** management is comfortable, but they gave **no concentration granularity** (broad vs. a few large levered accounts), and **customer bad debt ticked to $10M (from $1M)**. The +67% is the single biggest thing to keep interrogating.

### 🔵 CONNECTS DOTS — prediction markets pivoted to an *aggregator*, and it's really a weather/insurance play
- Launched **IBKR Prediction Markets**: a unified destination routing across **ForecastEx + CME + Kalshi** to the best net price. Milan: "our job is to offer connectivity and access… no reason to limit our clients to only ForecastEx… increase available liquidity… easier to attract institutions." **The pivot: from running an exchange (ForecastEx) to being the broker/router across all venues.**
- **Firmly reaffirmed: NO sports, NO entertainment contracts** — only events that can affect clients' portfolios. (Resolves the earlier ambiguity — management is unambiguous here.)
- **The tell (Budish):** ForecastEx volumes are **concentrated in a couple of temperature contracts.** Peterffy's expansion vision: **hurricane landfalls, "which… implies insurance risk."** So the grand "prediction markets" narrative is, in practice, becoming a **weather / insurance-risk-hedging venue** — not the election-markets story the headlines imply.

### 🟣 CURIOUS — agentic AI: clients are connecting ChatGPT/Claude/Grok to their accounts *organically*, and autonomous trading is on the roadmap
- **IBKR Connector** = enterprise integration with **OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI.** Milan: clients connected their chatbots to IBKR accounts **before any public announcement** (IBKR shows up in the chatbots' drop-downs); "very pleased."
- Today it's **"human-in-the-loop"**: the AI drafts an order → it appears on a UI → the client approves → it becomes an executable order. Growing usage.
- **Roadmap: fully autonomous agentic trading**, gated behind a client test on the "benefits and dangers." This is the concrete version of Peterffy's long-standing "AI raises trading velocity" thesis — a potential future **volume driver**, not just a cost story.

### 🔵 CONNECTS DOTS — IBKR is a quiet beneficiary of the China (Tiger/Futu) clampdown
- After the May Chinese-regulator clampdown on **Tiger and Futu** (attracting mainland accounts where unlicensed), Milan: **"a clear uptick in broker transfers… the number of accounts, and especially assets, has increased."** Much was **existing IBKR clients shifting assets away from Tiger/Futu onto IBKR.**
- IBKR stays compliant (no mainland advertising; verifies non-mainland residence). A **non-obvious tailwind** to the +40% client-equity / +27% credit-balance growth.

### 🟣 CURIOUS — Korea is a hit on semis, and overnight trading *nearly tripled* (last quarter's buried detail, confirmed)
- **First e-broker to offer Korea** (Korea Exchange + **Nextrade**, Korea's overnight ATS). "A line that goes straight up," semiconductor names most sought; the first Korean ADR US listing *added* to volumes.
- **Overnight trading nearly tripled YoY to 10.9M trades (from 3.8M).** The Q1 "curious" one-mention is now a **confirmed secular trend**, driven by international investors trading in "their waking hours."
- **Connect the dots:** semiconductors-as-market-drivers (Nancy's macro note) + Korea's overnight ATS + overnight tripling = **one international / 24-5 story.**

### 🟣 CURIOUS — one-third of crypto trading is already *perpetuals* (a recently-added product)
- Milan: perpetual futures solve crypto's short-selling and leverage problems; IBKR offers **Coinbase** perps; **"roughly one-third of the trading we see [in crypto] is now coming from these perpetuals."** Fast adoption; more venues to be added.

### 🟡 WATCH — capital is ballooning, and the growth may not be getting cheaper
- **Excess capital ~$10.3B** after buffers (Galik) — **up ~$1.1B QoQ**, and well above the "~$8B" cited a few months ago (and the ~$6–7B in older framing). **No buyback**; capital-return pressure builds.
- **M&A:** banks are pitching "dramatically" more targets — "a lot offered," nothing worth pursuing. Discipline intact ("not to say we don't look").
- **Two candid, thesis-tempering admissions:**
  - **Peterffy: marketing yield is NOT higher** — "roughly the same"; more spend, proportional yield, "but not more than proportionally higher." So the >30% account growth is **not** coming from structurally more efficient marketing.
  - **Milan: the "no dilution" in DARTs-per-account / commission-per-order is partly the strong environment** (volatility, SpaceX IPO), not proven structural durability.

### 📌 PROMISE UPDATES
- **OCC national trust bank charter:** preliminary conditional approval received; **"operational by year-end."** The Q1 silence is resolved with a concrete timeline. (Enables direct custody of mutual fund / ETF assets.)
- **Introducing-broker pipeline:** reconciled — **4th–5th straight quarter of double-digit integrations going online**, more in progress than last quarter, plus significant committed-but-not-started. **The *type* shifted** from startups to **established firms broadening their offering** (crypto/CFD providers adding listed stocks, retail brokers adding asset classes/regions, financial institutions onboarding to cut costs).
- **Section 31 fee (the "?" from Post-Results):** confirmed a pure pass-through — +$19M (to $34M total) from the SEC fee reinitiating (it had been $0 since mid-Q2 2025); grosses up both commission revenue and execution cost, **no profit impact** (margin held 77%). Ex-SEC-fees, execution/clearing was $108M vs $101M (+7%). Gross transactional profit margin 83%.

## The dots, connected

**The print confirmed the thesis; the call reframed the optionality.** NII proved the balance-offset works even as the Fed eases. Underneath, IBKR is turning its "broaden the box" bets into something bigger — **prediction markets as a weather/insurance-hedging aggregator, AI as a path to autonomous trading, and new venues (Korea / overnight) tied to a 24-5 international story** — while a **regulatory clampdown hands it Chinese-diaspora assets.** The only things to keep honest: **margin loans +67% earned just a "comfortable,"** and management was candid that the torrid growth **isn't getting cheaper to acquire.**
