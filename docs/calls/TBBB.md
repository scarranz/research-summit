# TBBB (BBB Foods / Tiendas 3B) — earnings-call record

The calls repository for TBBB per docs/EARNINGS_CONVENTIONS.md. Newest call on top. Raw transcripts
(the source PDFs / TXT) live outside the repo in `Desktop/Developer/TBBB/`; this file is the
distilled record that feeds the Earnings tab (`js/overviews/bbb.js`) and the Post-Results "Call
summary". Reporting is IFRS, fiscal year ends Dec 31; figures in Mexican pesos (Ps.) unless noted.

**Transcripts on file (7), all distilled below (newest first):** 2026-05-07 (1Q26) · 2026-03-12
(FY2025/4Q25) · 2025-11-20 (3Q25) · 2025-08-12 (2Q25) · 2025-05-08 (1Q25) · 2025-04-10 (FY2024/4Q24)
· 2024-11-26 (3Q24). Management is unchanged across all seven: **Anthony Hatoum** (Founder, Chairman
& CEO) and **Eduardo Pizzuto** (CFO); 2Q25 added Amparo Martínez (General Counsel) and Joaquín Ley (IR).

**The through-line, seven calls in one paragraph:** revenue has compounded **+30–38% every quarter**
on **same-store sales that climbed from ~+11.6% (3Q24) to a peak ~+17.9% (3Q25)** then settled ~+16%,
a gap to ANTAD that widened from ~9 to ~17 points; store openings **accelerated** (131 → 184 net/qtr;
FY24 484 → FY25 record 574); growth stayed **self-funded** on negative working capital (~10–11% of
revenue) and rising operating cash flow. The one recurring "read-it-adjusted" wrinkle is **EBITDA**:
a growing **non-cash share-based-compensation (SBC)** charge (plus a one-time asset write-off in 4Q25)
pushed *reported* EBITDA to a loss in 3Q25 and near-zero in 4Q25, while *adjusted* EBITDA kept
compounding (FY25 +30% to Ps.4.4B). Gross margin is deliberately **volatile / no target** (priced
SKU-by-SKU for peso margin × volume). Recurring debates: gross-margin normalization, ticket-vs-traffic,
SG&A leverage vs. the accelerating-openings drag, the peso/dollarized-cost passthrough (8–18 months),
and — newest — the **Aug 6, 2026 lock-up expiry**.

## 1Q26 — reported May 7, 2026

Participants: **Anthony Hatoum** (Founder, Chairman & CEO), **Eduardo Pizzuto** (CFO). Investors/
analysts only.

### The print (1Q26 earnings release)

- **Revenue Ps.23.0B (+33% YoY).**
- **Same-store sales +16%** vs 1Q25 — split **~2/3 volume** (transactions + SKUs per ticket) / **~1/3
  average price per SKU**, the price piece **mix-driven, not inflation** (internal inflation very low).
  SSS-vs-ANTAD gap **>14 points**.
- **Stores: +123 net new → 3,469 total** (LTM +580, ~+20% of the base); **20 distribution centers**.
- **Reported EBITDA Ps.554M**; **ex non-cash SBC, adjusted EBITDA +39% to Ps.1.3B**, adj. EBITDA
  margin **+22bps YoY**.
- **Operating cash flow Ps.2.0B (+64% YoY).**
- **Adjusted negative working capital Ps.9.4B** (vs Ps.6.5B in 2025 ex-IPO), ~**11.3% of LTM revenue**
  — the self-funding engine.
- Sales expenses **10.3% of revenue** (+5bps; utilities, permitting, higher D&A); admin ex-SBC
  ~unchanged. 1Q25 had a one-time Ps.54M secondary-follow-on expense.
- **Capex guided ~Ps.5.2B for FY2026** (stores + additional DCs + trucks/equipment).

### The call — the record

- **Growth is self-funded and durable:** "high-growth business model that is resilient and does very
  well across economic cycles… attractive unit economics, generates cash, becomes more competitive as
  it scales." Runway "very long"; long-term white space unchanged.
- **Margin:** commercial/gross-margin expansion from **product mix + supplier efficiencies** (stays
  quarter-to-quarter volatile); reiterated the **scale → purchasing power → part-margin / part-price
  "virtuous circle."**
- **SG&A leverage against the Mexico wage backdrop:** labor **fell as a % of revenue despite the
  minimum-wage increase**, on relentless "hours-worked" efficiency work at stores and DCs; the coming
  **work-week reduction (expected 2027)** played down as manageable. G&A ~stable this year as they add
  DCs + HQ talent; long-run G&A keeps declining as a % of revenue. No SG&A guidance given.
- **Talent / IT:** heavy reinvestment in talent — "we're an IT company selling groceries." **ERP is a
  3-year build, ~halfway through**, deployed gradually/modularly by region. **AI "starting to take
  root"** on efficiency/time gains.
- **Assortment / new categories:** ~**60 products/lines in test at any time**. **Fresh fruit &
  vegetable trial** shows a ticket uplift, likely to extend/retrofit. **Irrepetibles** ("treasure-
  hunt" spot buys — bicycles, white/brown goods, clothing) keep gaining share. Sweetened-beverage
  subcategory down on a **new January sweetener tax**, more than offset by non-sweetened.
- **Private label:** was 58% of sales last quarter (updated once a year), trend up; **no structural
  change** operating at higher penetration — cites **Bimbo as a "time machine"** for the end state.
- **Brand:** rising 3B brand recognition, tracked via **annual surveys (~15,000 customers/non-
  customers)**; near-zero marketing — word of mouth + social media; the "stretching" expansion means
  new regions already know the brand.

### Q&A — the record

- **Héctor Maya (Scotiabank):** competitor's March adjustments — any impact? "No." **Lock-up expires
  Aug 6, 2026** (the 20-F's "July 8" is an error they will **amend**) — the near-term share-overhang
  item. Follow-up: private-label penetration threshold — "no structural change" 50→60→70%.
- **Andrew Ruben (Morgan Stanley):** how brand recognition is measured (annual ~15k surveys) and how
  it travels into new regions (the gradual "stretch").
- **Bob Ford (BofA):** central-staff build-out & redundancy; ERP progress/deployment window;
  Irrepetibles + 2Q merchandising (Mother's Day, World Cup).
- **Alejandro Fuchs (Itaú BBA):** SSS traffic-vs-ticket split (2/3 vol · 1/3 price); new-store
  performance by geography — "very consistent across the board" (basic goods).
- **Gabriela Leme (Goldman Sachs):** SG&A under the minimum-wage increase & work-week reduction —
  leverage held; G&A ~stable this year.
- **Froylan Méndez (JPMorgan):** sources of gross-margin expansion (mix + supplier efficiencies);
  annual-survey findings (shifting consumption; brand tendencies favor 3B).
- **Antonio Hernández (Actinver):** best-performing categories (broad-based; sweetened beverages the
  one soft spot on the tax); pilots.
- **Joe Thomas (HSBC):** fresh trial specifics + ticket uplift; capex phasing (~Ps.5.2B, comfortable).
- **[Guli] / Federico Galassi (Rohatyn):** AI adoption inside 3B; whether the corporate structure is
  built to sustain growth — "planned ahead of time… in very good shape."

### Analysis — feeds the Earnings tab

**Scoring vs the operating story:** SSS durability **HELD** (+16%, >14pt ANTAD gap, mix not
inflation); margin leverage **HELD** (adj. EBITDA +39%, labor leverage through the wage hike);
self-funding **HELD** (neg. WC Ps.9.4B, OCF +64%). Reported vs adjusted EBITDA remains the one
"read-it-adjusted" line (SBC is the wedge).

**Into next quarter (2Q26 Watch List seeds):** (1) the **Aug 6 lock-up expiry** / share overhang;
(2) **gross-margin durability** (management flags it as volatile); (3) the **fresh / F&V rollout**
and Irrepetibles' share gains; (4) **G&A trajectory** as DCs + HQ talent are added; (5) **work-week-
reduction** cost prep into 2027; (6) private-label penetration at the annual update.

**Silences / to-watch:** no SG&A guidance; private-label % only updated annually; no explicit
2Q merchandising numbers; no quantified AI/ERP savings yet.

## FY2025 / 4Q25 — reported March 12, 2026

**The print.** Q4: **184 net new stores** (record FY total **574**, exceeding the 500–550 guide, +21% vs
484 in 2024); **SSS +16.6%** (Q4) / **+18.3%** (FY); **revenue Ps.22.0B (+34%)** Q4 / **Ps.78.0B (+36%)**
FY; Q4 **reported EBITDA Ps.79M**, but **ex non-cash SBC + a one-time asset write-off, +23% to Ps.1.2B**;
**FY reported EBITDA Ps.1.2B → ex-SBC/write-off +30% to Ps.4.4B**; **FY operating cash flow Ps.4.7B
(+~25%)**; 4 new DCs in 2025. 4-year revenue **CAGR 35%**; ANTAD gap **>15 pts**.

**The call.** Updated spaghetti chart (2005–2024 cohorts, inflation-adjusted): newer cohorts open higher
and ramp steeper, older cohorts still grow. Eduardo to detail the **asset write-off** and outline **2026
guidance**. The reported-vs-adjusted EBITDA wedge (SBC + write-off) is the quarter's one "read-it-clean"
line. **Watch seeds:** the 2026 store/capex guide, gross-margin normalization, the SBC schedule.

## 3Q25 — reported November 20, 2025

**The print.** **131 net new stores → 3,162**; **2 DCs → 18 total**; **LTM 528**; **SSS +17.9%** (the
peak of the series); **revenue Ps.20.3B (+36.7%)**; **reported EBITDA a LOSS of Ps.404M**, but **ex non-
cash SBC, +43.6% to +Ps.1.2B**; **9M operating cash flow Ps.3.0B (+30%)**; adjusted negative working
capital **Ps.7.8B (~10.8% of revenue**, vs Ps.5.4B in 3Q24 ex-IPO). ANTAD gap **~17 pts** — the widest.

**The call.** First quarter where **reported EBITDA went negative** purely on the non-cash SBC charge —
management leaned hard on the adjusted figure and the appendix schedule. Older vintages' EBITDA margins
"close to other hard discounters"; ~half the base opened in the last three years (the consolidated-
leverage drag). Reiterated **white space of no less than 14,000 stores**. Sales expenses 10.1%→10.2%.

## 2Q25 — reported August 12, 2025

**The print.** **142 net new stores → 3,031**; **1H 259** (vs 215); **LTM 528** (vs 460); **SSS +17.7%**
(vs +10.7% in 2Q24); **revenue Ps.18.8B (+38.3%)**; **reported EBITDA Ps.844M (+22.5%)**, **ex-SBC +32%**;
**1H operating cash flow Ps.1.9B (+56%)**; ANTAD gap **15 pts**. Sales expenses 10.4%→10.5%; admin +31bps
(3.6%→3.9%, incl **Ps.111M** non-cash SBC).

**The call.** Welcomed two hires — **Amparo Martínez (General Counsel)** and **Joaquín Ley (IR)**. SBC
detail: a **legacy 20-year plan terminated at IPO** + a **new standard post-IPO plan**, and a **June board
IPO-tied award** that **does not change the fully-diluted share count** (already factored) — it just books a
non-cash expense on grant. **45% of the base opened in the last three years**, so unit-level leverage is
real but masked at the consolidated level by the opening pace.

## 1Q25 — reported May 8, 2025

**The print.** **117 net new stores → 2,889** (vs 94 in 1Q24); **LTM 507** (vs 416); **SSS +13.5%**;
**revenue Ps.17.1B (+35.1%)**; **reported EBITDA Ps.705M (+12.7%)**, margin 4.9%→4.1% on growth
investment; **operating cash flow Ps.1.1B (+49%)**; adjusted negative working capital **Ps.6.5B
(~10.5% of revenue**, vs Ps.4.8B in Mar-2024). Net cash ~Ps.1.6B + $150M IPO cash.

**The call.** Sales expenses 10.2%→10.3% and admin +60bps (3.5%→4.1%, incl **Ps.84M** SBC ~50bps) — both
the accelerating-openings drag and deliberate reinvestment: **hiring for four new 2025 regions** and
raising talent density at HQ (**IT, purchasing, controls, legal**). "We pay the full cost of new stores
in regions before we see the full revenues" — the clearest framing of the consolidated-leverage optics.

## FY2024 / 4Q24 — reported April 10, 2025

**The print.** Q4: **138 net new stores**; **SSS +11.8%** (vs ANTAD **2.6%**); **revenue Ps.16.3B (+32.7%)**.
FY2024: **484 net new stores (+21% vs 2023)**; **SSS +13.4%**; **revenue Ps.57.4B (+30.3%)**; **operating

**The call.** First **spaghetti chart** post-IPO (2005–2023 cohorts, inflation-adjusted): newer cohorts
start higher and ramp faster, no curve has flattened. For 5-year-plus stores, **traffic +4.6%** and
**ticket +3.6%** — growth from both, private-label-led. Eduardo outlined **2025 guidance**. "When the
economy slows and cost of living rises, that tends to play in our favor."

## 3Q24 — reported November 26, 2024

**The print.** **131 net new stores → 2,634** (Sep-30-2024); **9M 346 net** (+42% vs 243); **SSS +11.6%**;
**revenue Ps.14.8B (+29.8%)**; **EBITDA Ps.688M (+54%** from Ps.447M**)**; gross margin **15.8% (flat YoY**;
prior quarter had been 16.7%); **SG&A 13.4% of revenue (−51bps** from 13.9%); **9M operating cash flow
Ps.2.3B (+22.4%)**; adjusted negative working capital **10.3% of revenue**. FY2024 store guidance 380–420
(to "solidly meet").

**The call.** ~**1,500 Neto stores** sit next to a 3B; asked about Neto's rumored supplier-payment troubles,
Hatoum declined to tease out a "Neto effect" ("walk into a 3B and a Neto and you'll see"). **Gross margin
has no target** — priced SKU-by-SKU for volume × peso margin, so it fluctuates; the model "keeps gross
margin flat forever" paired with a store sales curve. Price changes show up with an **8–18-month lag**;
dollarized input costs pass through over the same window on peso weakness. Unit-economics target **capex
~Ps.3.9M per store**; **dividends "way too early."** Long-term white space cited at **up to 20,000 stores**.
