## Q3 2026 — October 22, 2026

Participants: Bryan Goldberg (Head of IR), Alex Norström and Gustav Söderström (Co-CEOs), Christian Luiga (CFO). Q&A at
8:00am ET before the market open, questions through Slido (S-L-I-D-O dot com, code #SpotifyEarningsQ326).

### The print (Q3 2026 Update)

- MAU **788M** (guide 788M, +11M, +11% YoY); Premium subscribers **306M** (guide 305M, +6M, +9%); Ad-Supported MAU 499M.
- Revenue **€4,969M** (+16%, +14% cc) against a ~€5.0B guide; Premium €4,487M (+17%, +15% cc); Ad-Supported €482M (+10%, +9% cc).
  Premium ARPU **€4.95** (+9.3%, +7.5% cc). 3Q25 restated comparatives: Premium €3,835M, Ad-Supported €437M.
- Gross profit €1,639M, margin **33.0%** (guide 32.9%, +136bps YoY); Premium 34.5%, Ad-Supported 18.7%.
- Operating income **€672M** (guide €670M, 13.5% margin); social charges €22M against €9M embedded; operating expenses €967M (+26%, +19% ex-FX
  and social charges). Net income €518M, diluted EPS €2.48 (diluted shares 208.6M).
- Free cash flow **€735M** (−9% YoY; operating cash flow €740M, capex €6M); LTM €3.2B; cash, restricted cash and short term investments €9.9B,
  no debt other than leases. $1.1B repurchased year to date through Oct 21 (2.2M shares).
- **Q4 2026 guide:** MAU 818M (+30M), subscribers 316M (+10M), revenue €5.2B (ARPU +4.5% to 5% YoY; ~230bps FX tailwind on USD:EUR 0.8828),
  gross margin 33.8%, operating income €820M (with €6M of social charges at a $487.38 close).
