### 3.1 How the artifact models members (Nov 10, 2026)

- **2026E base: 2.51M at year-end.** This is the BBG estimate; Oscar gives no member guidance. It replaces the 2.69M Sep-30 count, which overstated the base because members keep leaving in 4Q (eAPTC expiry, plus the second CMS eligibility review of ~500k lives).
- **2027 member growth, set by case:** 0% (Low), 3% (Mid), 6% (High). This is our reading of a market Oscar sees stable at ~17M in 2027. 2027 growth comes mainly from rate (~14% filed). BBG has +3.3%.
- **2028–29: backed out of the revenue CAGR for each case** (21%, 24%, 27%, from the slide 44 walk).

| Case | Members 2027 | Growth 2028–29 / yr | Members 2029 | EPS 2027 | EPS 2029 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 2.51M | 19.0% | 3.55M | $2.70 | $3.38 |
| Mid | 2.59M | 20.5% | 3.75M | $3.33 | $4.68 |
| High | 2.66M | 22.0% | 3.96M | $4.02 | $5.88 |
| Consensus-like (BBG) | 2.60M | 10.2% / 19.7% | 3.43M | $2.61 | $4.08 |

**Takeaways:**
- Against the footprint build of 3.8–4.5M (§3), only High (3.96M) is inside; Low (3.55M) and Mid (3.75M) sit just below the floor. The 18% × 23M ≈ 4.1M national cross-check sits mid-range.
- A 21–27% revenue CAGR with premium per member at only +5–7% requires ~19–22% member growth a year in 2028–29. That is the weak point of the target: it depends on the market returning to ~23M and on Oscar gaining share in exactly those two years.

## 7. Margin walk, below the line, capital

1. 2026E: **3.2–4.2%**.

### 9.1 BBG's line items do not tie to each other (found Nov 10, 2026)

Each BBG line is the mean of whichever brokers submit that line, so the lines don't add up:

| 2029E | Value | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| MLR field (`MGD_CARE_MEDICAL_CARE_RATIO`) | 80.1% | — |
| MLR from gross profit: (revenue − GP) ÷ premium | **78.7%** | the MLR behind BBG's operating income |
| Total revenue vs premium + investment + service | 30,854 vs 30,836 | gap $17M (2026 $39M, **2027 $394M**, 2028 $180M) |
| Net income ÷ diluted shares | 1,432 ÷ 350.9 = **$4.08** | — |
| Diluted EPS, GAAP field | **$4.18** | — |
| Diluted EPS, adjusted field | **$4.01** | the number usually quoted as "consensus" |

2027 goes the other way: the gross profit implies an MLR of 82.4%, while the MLR field shows 81.2%.

Effect on the artifact:
- **Consensus-like** used to take the MLR field and gave 2029 EPS of $3.10.
- It now uses the MLR implied by gross profit, with SG&A = GP − operating income − D&A, BBG's tax expense ÷ pre-tax, and BBG's shares, members, premium and investment income.
- The revenue gap goes into other revenues.
- Result: revenue ties exactly, net income lands at $1,433M against BBG's $1,432M, and EPS is **$4.08**.
- The table and the valuation show both BBG EPS fields.
