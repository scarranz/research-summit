### Prepared remarks — the record (VERBATIM, primary source)

**The AV thesis, and the line that carries it:**
> *"The vehicle itself is just the start of the total cost of an AV fleet. The rest — charging,
> maintenance, cleaning, depot infrastructure, fleet orchestration — is operations. Most have to pay
> someone else to do that work. We don't. Flexdrive has spent a decade and built dozens of
> facilities doing this. **Nashville isn't where we're learning how to do this, it's where we are
> starting to commercialize it.**"*

> *"The industry conversation around AVs has shifted — away from whether the technology works, and
> toward who can deploy it… Do we have the demand platform, the fleet management infrastructure, and
> depot capabilities? Check, check, check."*

> *"A hybrid model - fixed capacity from AVs, elastic capacity from human drivers - is how we are
> building a business for the next fifteen years."*

**The three dated Nashville/Waymo milestones** (the most scoreable promises on the call):
> - *"This summer we will be taking on the management of fleet operations, facilities, and charging infrastructure."*
> - *"This fall we will open our 80,000 square-foot depot."*
> - ***"In 2H:26, riders will also be able to use the Lyft app to match with a Waymo vehicle."***

**Capital returns:**
> *"During the quarter, we repurchased approximately $300 million in shares, **taking an
> opportunistic approach to capital return given what we viewed as a dislocation in our share
> price.** For 2026, we expect buybacks at a similar level to 2025, while preserving flexibility to
> invest in growth opportunities."*

⚠ **Tension worth flagging:** $300M in Q1 alone against full-year guidance of "similar to 2025"
(~$500M) implies management expects to slow the pace materially over the remaining three quarters.

**Margins / insurance:**
> *"Gross margin expanded year over year driven by a reduction in our average insurance cost per ride
> aided by recent insurance reforms and advancement of our insurance strategies."*

**Competition (Lyft never says "Uber" — it is "the other guys"):**
> *"When dual-app drivers were asked in our most recent survey which rideshare app they prefer, 58%
> answered Lyft, a 38-percentage point advantage to the other guys."* (up from a 31-point advantage
> cited on the Q4 call)

> *"A study by the National Bureau of Economic Research highlights the cost of being on autopilot: a
> NYC rider taking 100 rides in 2024 would have saved an estimated $177 that year by checking both
> apps. When we say Check Lyft, this is what we mean."*

**Europe — the Gett close:**
> *"This week, we closed our acquisition of Gett's UK business… Gett is one of London's leading black
> cab apps with strong B2B relationships and will nearly double the number of rides on the Lyft
> platform in London."* — and financially: *"Although strategically material, this will be
> financially immaterial to the quarter."*

> *"Freenow's decade of relationships with local governments… gives Lyft a structural advantage that
> would take years to replicate."*

**High-value modes:** *"high-value mode rides were up 35% year over year and delivered **margins more
than double a standard ride**. High-value modes still only represent a single-digit percent of our
Rides."*

**Partnerships:** *"Nearly 27% of Rides in North America are linked to a partnership — an all-time
high."* United MileagePlus at ~300M miles with pay-with-miles launched (*"an industry first, only on
Lyft"*). Canada rides +~50% YoY.

### Said on the call but NOT in the press release

The Q1 release was thin — it carried **no** mention of buybacks, Lyft Silver, Lyft Media, Waymo, or
any AV partner. Call-only material therefore includes: the entire $300M buyback and its "dislocation"
rationale; the insurance-driven gross-margin bridge; the three dated Nashville milestones; the 58% /
38-point driver preference; a 3-million-ride weather impact (winter storm Hernando); the NBER study;
the Hamburg first-mile/last-mile pilot and *"we are finalizing our vehicle partner"*; Lyft Maps
mapping starting in Barcelona; **the McDonald's ad campaign launching that day**, the Trade Desk
audience-extension product, and that **Suzie Reider (ex-YouTube) runs the ad group**; Nashville hiring
colour (*"50% [of Nashville roles filled] were current or former Lyft drivers"*); and the fuel relief
program's scale (*"almost a dollar in savings"*, *"not material to our overall financial profile"*).
